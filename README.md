# Customer Shopping Trends Analysis | Beginner SQL Project

## Project Overview
This project analyzes customer shopping behavior for a retail clothing store using SQL. The goal is to identify purchasing trends, customer preferences, and seasonal demand patterns to support better inventory and business decisions.

The dataset used for this project was sourced from Kaggle.

---

## Business Problem
Retail businesses often face challenges such as:

- Overstocking low-demand products
- Running out of high-demand inventory
- Understanding customer preferences across seasons and locations

This project focuses on solving these challenges through SQL-based data analysis.

---

## Dataset Information
The dataset includes information related to:

- Customer demographics
- Product purchases
- Seasonal shopping trends
- Store locations
- Customer ratings
- Purchase history
- Spending behavior

---

## Analysis Questions

### Customer Analysis
- How many unique customers visited the store?
- What percentage of customers are male vs. female?
- Should the store focus more on male or female clothing inventory?

### Seasonal Trend Analysis
- What seasons are represented in the dataset?
- Which product categories perform best in each season?
- Which items are most purchased by season?
- What colors are most popular during different seasons?

### Location-Based Analysis
- Do customer preferences vary by store location?
- Should stocking strategies differ across regions?
- Which locations show stronger customer engagement and satisfaction?

### Customer Loyalty Analysis
- Do repeat customers spend more than first-time customers?
- Is there a relationship between previous purchases and total spending?
- Which customer groups generate the highest business value?
