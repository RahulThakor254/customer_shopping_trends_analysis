# Insights Summary

## Customer Demographics Analysis

The dataset included a total of **3,900 unique customers**.

Gender distribution analysis showed:
- **68% Male customers**
- **32% Female customers**

This indicates that male shoppers represented the majority of purchases during the analyzed period.

### Business Insight
The store should maintain stronger inventory levels for male-focused categories while continuing to stock female products strategically to serve both customer segments effectively.

---

## Seasonal Shopping Trends Analysis

The dataset included purchasing activity across:
- Winter
- Spring
- Summer
- Fall

Customer buying behavior changed noticeably across different seasons.

### Most Purchased Items by Season

| Season | Top Purchased Items |
|---|---|
| Fall | Jackets, Hats, Handbags, Skirts |
| Winter | Sunglasses, Pants, Shirts |
| Spring | Sweaters, Shorts, Blouses |
| Summer | Pants, Dresses, Jewelry |

### Business Insight
Seasonal demand patterns show that inventory planning should change throughout the year instead of following a fixed stocking strategy.

Examples:
- Outerwear products performed strongly during colder seasons
- Lightweight clothing and accessories increased during warmer seasons

This helps reduce overstocking and improve product availability during peak demand periods.

---

## Product Color Preference Analysis

Customer color preferences varied by season, showing that fashion trends and seasonal behavior influence purchasing decisions.

Some colors consistently performed better during specific seasons, especially for clothing and accessories categories.

### Business Insight
Retailers should consider seasonal color trends while purchasing inventory and designing promotional campaigns.

Aligning products with seasonal preferences can improve customer engagement and sales performance.

---

## Location-Based Shopping Analysis

Customer preferences differed significantly across store locations.

### Example: Montana

During Fall, customers in Montana showed stronger purchasing demand for:
- Handbags
- Sweaters
- T-Shirts

These purchasing patterns differed from overall national trends.

### Business Insight
Different locations may require different inventory strategies.  
Using region-specific stocking plans can improve inventory efficiency and customer satisfaction.

---

## Customer Experience Analysis

Store performance was evaluated using average customer review ratings.

### Top Performing Locations
- Texas
- Wisconsin

Both locations recorded average review ratings above **3.88**.

### Business Insight
High-performing locations may have stronger customer service practices, inventory management, or shopping experiences.

Retailers can study these locations and apply similar operational strategies to lower-performing stores.

---

## Customer Loyalty & Spending Analysis

The analysis showed that customers with more than **10 previous purchases** consistently spent more than customers with fewer purchases.

This indicates a positive relationship between repeat purchasing behavior and customer spending value.

### Business Insight
Repeat customers represent an important revenue-driving segment for the business.

Strategies such as:
- Loyalty programs
- Personalized promotions
- Repeat customer discounts
- Membership rewards

can help improve customer retention and long-term profitability.

---

# Final Recommendations

Based on the SQL analysis, the following business recommendations were identified:

- Adjust inventory based on seasonal purchasing trends
- Use location-specific stocking strategies instead of a single national inventory model
- Increase focus on high-performing product categories during peak seasons
- Improve customer retention through loyalty-focused marketing strategies
- Analyze successful store locations and apply their operational practices across other regions

Overall, the analysis demonstrates how SQL can be used to generate practical retail business insights from customer shopping data.
